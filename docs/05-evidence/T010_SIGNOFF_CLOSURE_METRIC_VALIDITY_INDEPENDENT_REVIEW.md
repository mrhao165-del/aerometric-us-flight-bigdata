## 3. Analysis & Invariant Verification

### 3.1 Strict Absence of Nulls, NaNs, and Infs
Under the curated acceptance criteria of Gate G4 and G5, rows admitted to `data/curated/` must possess complete, mathematically valid values for all operational measures. The independent PyArrow scan confirms:
- **Zero Nulls:** Across all 38,765,909 measure data points ($5,537,987 \times 7$), not a single null was encountered.
- **Zero NaNs:** IEEE 754 NaN values are completely absent.
- **Zero Infinities:** No infinite floats exist in the physical Parquet data.

### 3.2 Range & Boundary Compliance
The empirical minimum and maximum bounds observed in the real 2016 dataset fall completely within realistic operational boundaries:
1. **Delays (`ARR_DELAY`, `DEP_DELAY`):**
   - Minimum arrival delay of -152.0 minutes (flights arriving ahead of schedule).
   - Maximum arrival delay of 2,142.0 minutes (~35.7 hours, extreme severe weather or mechanical disruption).
   - Minimum departure delay of -204.0 minutes (early pushbacks).
   - Maximum departure delay of 2,149.0 minutes (~35.8 hours).
2. **Taxi Times (`TAXI_OUT`, `TAXI_IN`):**
   - Minimum taxi times are $\ge 1.0$ minute, confirming positive non-zero physical movement.
   - Maximum taxi-out of 186.0 minutes (~3.1 hours, severe ground hold/de-icing queues).
   - Maximum taxi-in of 250.0 minutes (~4.1 hours, post-landing ramp congestion).
3. **Flight Durations (`AIR_TIME`, `ACTUAL_ELAPSED_TIME`, `CRS_ELAPSED_TIME`):**
   - Shortest air time is 4.0 minutes (ultra-short inter-island or reposition hops).
   - Longest air time is 723.0 minutes (~12 hours, long-haul continental-Hawaii routes).
   - Scheduled elapsed time bounds ($[18.0, 705.0]$) and actual elapsed time bounds ($[14.0, 778.0]$) exhibit full physical plausibility.
